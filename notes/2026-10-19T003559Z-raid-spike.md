## run1 (2026-10-19 00:25:51Z to 00:32:50Z): a planner bug

- Disks: vda (virtio, 4G), and sda and sdb (`scsi-hd` rotation 7200, 2G).
  Because of U2, all three are HDDs.
- Result: `STATE=applied DETAIL=auto-applied`, but VGs were `large` (vda1) and
  `swap` (md0). **There was no `main` and no `slash`**, and `large/data`
  was mounted on `/mnt/data`.
- Filed as trbarbour/NixOS-boot-image#313. Root cause, from the code: without
  an SSD, `main` and `swap` both chose the RAID-1 pair, and `swap` took
  precedence. Also, the swap-room check compared bytes with GiB
  (`min_size < 2 * ram_gb`), so it never excluded anything.
- Fix: `main` is chosen first and its bucket is left out of the swap
  candidates; the comparison is now in bytes; a postcondition asserts that
  `main/slash` exists. New tests are in `tests/test_planner_hdd_only.py`
  (3 of 4 failed on the old code).
- The existing `test_multiple_hdd_buckets_named_separately` expected the
  buggy plan, with no `slash`. It was corrected to reqs §2.4.
